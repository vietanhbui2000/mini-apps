Add a widget to your notes

Notion

  1. Choose Copy link.
  2. In a Notion page, type /embed, paste the link and choose Embed link.
  3. Drag the embed’s edges to size it. The widget fills whatever space it gets.

Obsidian

  1. Choose Copy code.
  2. Paste it into a note. It shows in Reading view and Live Preview.

The settings live in the link, so you can edit it by hand or come back here. Auto theme follows your device’s light or dark setting; if your notes app uses a different one, pick Light or Dark.
